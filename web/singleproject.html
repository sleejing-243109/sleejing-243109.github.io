“Dawn FM”

– THE WEEKND

Synth-Pop / Dance-Pop / R&B

Cover Concept:

An aged, bearded version of The Weeknd in a dark, shadowy setting, with a single ray of light shining over his shoulder — symbolizing themes of mortality, transition, and an otherworldly journey.

Theme:

The album is framed like a psychedelic radio station broadcast — “103.5 Dawn FM” — acting as a guide through a purgatory-like space.

Themes include existential reflection, life and death, and the idea of waiting for the light at the end of a tunnel.
