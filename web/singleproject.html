“Dawn FM”

– THE WEEKND

Synth-Pop / Dance-Pop / R&B

Cover Concept:

An aged, bearded version of The Weeknd in a dark, shadowy setting, with a single ray of light shining over his shoulder — symbolizing themes of mortality, transition, and an otherworldly journey.

Theme:

The album is framed like a psychedelic radio station broadcast — “103.5 Dawn FM” — acting as a guide through a purgatory-like space.

Themes include existential reflection, life and death, and the idea of waiting for the light at the end of a tunnel.

Behind-the-Scenes

Step into the studio where Midnight Remedy was born. From late-night writing sessions to intimate vocal takes, this track was crafted through emotion, experimentation, and pure artistic chemistry. We explored moody synths, soft percussive layers, and delicate vocal textures to shape its elegant nighttime atmosphere. Every harmony, every pause, every note was intentionally designed to capture the feeling of drifting through quiet city lights at midnight.
