ABOUT THE ARTIST

TYLER,THE CREATOR

BIO

Tyler, The Creator (born Tyler Gregory Okonma; March 6, 1991) is an American rapper, singer, songwriter, record producer, and fashion designer. Known for his creative, boundary-pushing music, he blends hip-hop, alternative, and experimental sounds. Tyler is recognized for his unique voice, distinctive visuals, and artistic versatility.

Vocals:

Rap, Hip-Hop,R&B

Skills:

Songwriting, Composing, Arranging
